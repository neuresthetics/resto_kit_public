# Encircle Tech Sheet: Daily Work (iPad)

Unofficial. Current Encircle Hydro, from the help center (checked Oct 18, 2026). MC = moisture content. [verify] = check in app.

## Before you go

- Offline prep: **Claim** > **☰** > **Download Data**
  while you have signal

## Open the job

- Find it: **My Claims** > search name, claim #, address
- Open Hydro: **Claim** > **Hydro** > **Tasks** tile
  (**Visit #**)

## Daily moisture readings

- Reading: **Moisture Content Readings** > room > **Tap
  to take reading** > **Add Meter Photo** > shutter >
  **Done** > check MC > **Save**
- **Wettest spot of that material in the area today (it
  can move). Take the meter photo.**
- New wet material? Tell your lead (new point)

## Air readings, every visit (all 4)

- Outside: **Exterior Reading** > **Add Meter Photo** >
  Temp + RH > **Save** (or **Use** weather)
- Unaffected: **Unaffected Area Reading** > room > meter
  photo > Temp + RH > **Save**
- Affected: **Affected Area Reading** (each chamber, same
  room) > meter photo > Temp + RH > **Save**
- Each dehu: **Dehumidifier Readings** > room > **Tap to
  take reading** > **Add Meter Photo** + **Add Timer
  Photo** > Temp, RH, Hours Running > **Save**
- **No chamber = no affected or dehu tasks. Ask your lead
  to set one up.**

## Equipment

- Add: **Equipment Placement** > red **+** > room > **+**
  per type > **Place** [verify on later visits]
- Pull one: **Hydro** > **Equipment** > **Days On Site**
  > **⋯** > **Remove From Job** > **Yes**
- Pull all: **Equipment** > **⋯** (top right) > **Remove
  All Equipment** > **Yes**
- **Pull it in the app the same day you pull it on site**

## Finish the visit

- Done: task list > **Complete**

## Photos

- Overviews: room > **+** > **Add Photos & Videos** >
  **Done**
- Before: overviews before you touch anything, left to
  right
- After / stages: **+** > **Add Room Note** > "Demo
  complete" + photos > **Save**
- Wrong room: **Select** > **Move** > structure > room
- **Real room names. Never rooms called "Before" or "Day
  2"**

## Notes

- Room note: room > **+** > **Add Room Note** > title >
  photos > **Save**
- Job-wide: claim home > **General Notes** > red **+**

## Sync and offline

- Offline: **Network** icon > **Offline Mode**
- Sync: **Network** icon > **Wi-Fi + Mobile Data**, app
  open, screen on
- Done when: no number on the **Network** icon
- Stuck: **Offline Mode** 10 s, then back
- **Never delete or reinstall with unsynced data**
- Support: 1-888-937-1077 x1
