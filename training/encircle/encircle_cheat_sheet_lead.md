# Encircle PM / Lead Sheet: Setup and Oversight

Unofficial. Current Encircle Hydro, from the help center (checked Oct 18, 2026). MC = moisture content. [verify] = check in app.

## Techs

- *Techs use the Tech sheet for daily work. This sheet is
  setup and oversight.*

## Hydro Job Setup

- Open: **Claim** > **Hydro** > **Tasks** > **Start
  Here**
- Loss: **Source of Loss and Category of Water** >
  category > **Complete**
- Chamber: **Drying Chamber** > rooms,
  **Affected**/**Unaffected** > **Done** > name >
  **Complete Task**
- **Chamber first, then psychrometrics. It unlocks the
  chamber tasks.**
- Air: **Exterior Reading**, **Unaffected Area Reading**
  (no chamber needed); **Affected Area Reading** per
  chamber; **Dehumidifier Readings** each dehu

## Materials and dry standards

- Dry standard: **Dry Standard** > material > Meter
  **Change** > **Change Meter** > **Add Meter Photo** >
  check MC > **Drying Variance** (10%) > **Save**
- Not listed: **+ Add** on the material list
- Next material: red **+** on the Dry Standard list
- *One per wet material, read in a dry area. Hydro
  remembers the meter.*

## Moisture points (no map)

- First: **Moisture Points** > **Place moisture points**
  (by room) > material > **Done**
- More: tap the room > red **+** > **Moisture Point** >
  material > **Done**
- **One point per affected material, per room**
- *Map only if a carrier/job asks: **Room Moisture Map**
  > **Place** > **Place Existing** > **Place All***

## Equipment count and settings

- Count: **Room & Affected Area Dimensions**, then
  **Equipment Calculation** > **Calculate**
- Settings: **Drying Chamber Settings** >
  **Stabilization**/**Drying**/**Dry Confirmed** >
  **Save**
- Later: **Hydro** > chamber > **⋯** > **Manage Drying
  Chamber**
- Past visit: **Tasks** > **⋯** > **Change Visit**

## Floor Plan

- Scan: **Claim** > **Add Floor Plans** > **Let's Scan**
  > red **record**
- Access: Standard/Admin users (not Limited)
- Sketch: plan > **Create Sketch** > name > **Save** >
  crop > **Save** > room > **Save**
- Fix size: plan > **wrench** > describe > **Send**

## Forms and signatures

- On site: **Documents** > **Generate Report** > **PDF
  Report** > form
- Remote: **Share** > **Add Link** > email/SMS > docs >
  **Preview & Sign** > **Share**

## Reports

- Moisture: **Documents** > **Generate Report** > **PDF
  Report** > **Hydro Full/Summary/Equipment/Carrier
  Report** [verify on mobile]
- Job: **Encircle PDF Report** > **Everything** > **Show
  Psychrometrics & Drying Information**: **Yes** >
  **Save**

## Xactimate import

- Import: **Data Request** > **Interior Walls** > Floor
  Plan ID as **Claim Number** > **Submit** > Sketch >
  **Data Import** > **Import**
- *Integration must be on. $10 per import; ceiling comes
  in at 8 ft.*
