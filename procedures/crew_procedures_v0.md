# Crew Procedures (v0, crew version)

> Generic v0 draft. Adapt to your company's procedures, scope sheet and local rules.

**Approach:** We're rolling out a lot at once, so the crew version stays basic, general, and in plain language. Nobody can absorb a phone book on day one.

The goal is to get habits in motion, not tool each step to the extreme. A parallel technical companion (same numbering) carries the high-detail version with RestoKit applied, for leads and PMs.

## 1. Field Documentation (v0)

### 1.1 Get to know the job (lead, first crew visit)

Before demo, open the Encircle job and look over the moisture map. Then take a few minutes to walk the water with your meter and find its edges yourself.

Knowing where the wet stops lets you cut only what needs to go, so you don't take too much or leave wet stuff behind. It's quick, it's one of the better parts of the job, and it builds the eye you'll need to move up.

If anything looks bigger or different than the map, tell the PM before demo.

### 1.2 Arrival and departure sets

Demo days and any day the layout changes.

- Every affected room from the corners, inside out.
- Clean up before the departure set.
- Each departure set becomes the next day's "before," and it shows equipment placement and airflow, so there's no separate equipment shot.
- **Monitoring visits:** dehu readout and readings are enough.
- Inspections are readings, tape, and mapping only.

### 1.3 Proof photos

Photograph anything that will be hidden or hauled away by the end of the job:

- insulation in walls
- wet pad and subfloor
- mold or rot
- damage behind cabinets
- the cause of loss
- anything an adjuster might say wasn't there.

Also the front of the house and action shots. Wide first, then close.

**Meter shots:** screen and material in the same frame.

### 1.4 Measurements

The sketch covers routine footage. Tape-in-frame shot only when the sketch can't show it:

- a non-standard cut
- an odd-shaped removal area
- or anything likely to be disputed.

### 1.5 Daily scope sheet

One per job per day, or one per room on big jobs.

The sheet is the list of what we bill, so fill it out as you go. **If it's on the sheet, photograph it.**

Finish the checklist at the bottom before you leave.

### 1.6 Dehus

- Reset job hours at placement.
- Log unit number, placement and pull dates in Encircle.
- Photo of readout and unit number at placement, daily, and at pull.
- Fans and air scrubbers aren't tracked per unit.

### 1.7 Equipment numbers

Large on the top and front, so nobody has to flip a unit.

### 1.8 Broken equipment

Tape on it: a few words on the problem, the date, your initials, before it goes back to the shop.

## 2. Cleaning (v0)

**How to think about it.** Look at what's dirty and where it is, check what tools you have, and take the path that gets the cleanest result in the time you've got.

Work from the top down, and move dirt toward the floor instead of chasing it with a rag. Do the best job you can with the time you have.

### 2.1 Every visit

- Pick up, bag trash, sweep or vac walkways.
- Leave the site clean, then take the departure photos.

### 2.2 Standard post-demo cleaning (base, end of demo day)

1. **HEPA.** Vacuum up the loose debris.
2. **Dry blow-down.** With the air scrubber running, blow dust off ledges, door frames, tops and other horizontal surfaces down to the floor, inside your demo setup. The ULV fogger with the liquid off makes a great air jet for this; a fan or leaf blower works too.
3. **HEPA.** Vacuum up what came down while it's still dry.
4. **Damp wipe.** Mist with a pump sprayer and wipe, or use a wet rag, whichever fits your tools. You're only picking up dust now, not dirt, so one rag goes ten times further.
5. **HEPA.** Vacuum the floor last.

It's less work than HEPA, wipe, HEPA and gets a better result.

### 2.3 Adjust to the job

- Extra dusty, or the air still hazy? A light ULV fog helps.
- Use antimicrobial for mold, Category 2 or 3 water, or anything with legal or insurance stakes.
- Whatever you do goes on the scope sheet.

### 2.4 Final check (before we call it done)

Take a close look and swipe a few spots with a rag. If it's dirty, clean it again. Take a photo, then call it.

> **Tip:** 20 minutes of plastic saves an hour of cleaning. Hate cleaning? Containment is your friend.

Air-test (IEP) jobs get the strict procedure in the technical companion.

## 3. Crew Training (v0)

Short, hands-on sessions in the shop or on a job.

- **3.1 Dehu setup:** reset job hours, set the humidistat, find the unit number, read the screen for the photo.
- **3.2 Scope sheet and photos:** fill out the sheet, finish the bottom checklist, photograph what's on it. Wide, then close, meter in frame.
- **3.3 Getting to know the water:** walk a real job with a lead and find the edges.
- **3.4 Broken equipment tag:** tape, problem, date, initials.
- **3.5 Cleaning:** the after-demo minimum (HEPA, blow-down, HEPA, damp wipe, HEPA), when to use antimicrobial, final check, plastic tip.
- **3.6 Arrival and departure sets:** when to take them, from the corners, clean up first.
