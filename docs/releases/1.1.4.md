## Weather-related water and category

- Weather-related water (wind-driven rain, storm water, roof and window leaks) is not Category 3 because of how it got in. The category is set from what the inspection finds: the source, the path the water took, how long it sat, the temperature, and any sign of contamination.
- Storm water that the inspection shows is badly contaminated (sewage, soil, silt, debris, strong odor, or growth) is still Category 3. Do not under-call it.
- Staining from rain running over wood or framing is not proof of contamination and not proof of rot. Meter it and probe it before calling removal. A whole-house rain loss can show wide staining with sound wood underneath.
- Removal and cut height follow the written category and the wet readings. There is no automatic flood cut for storm water.

## Dry standard and drying goal at the first visit

- At the first visit, the unaffected readings set the dry standard for each affected material, and the drying goal is written into the file then, so every visit and the final check are measured against it.
