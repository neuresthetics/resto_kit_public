# RestoKit 1.1.6 (2026-11-21)

Replaces 1.1.5 as the live kit. The previous version stays downloadable for a while.

Live file: `resto_kit_1.1.6.json` (`meta.version` 1.1.6). Sign up for the kit at [resto-kit.com/get-the-kit](https://resto-kit.com/get-the-kit). Agents should check [resto-kit.com/kit/latest.json](https://resto-kit.com/kit/latest.json) on first load in a session.

This page is the public update record and change log for 1.1.6. There are no new field rules in this release.

## Acronyms

- Common trade acronyms a tech should know, such as HEPA, PPE, and HVAC, are fine when the Glossary defines them. Obscure or company-specific acronyms are spelled out or avoided. Section ids are not affected.
- The Glossary has 48 new plain-words entries, so every trade acronym the kit uses is defined. Examples: PPE (personal protective equipment), HVAC (heating, ventilation, and air conditioning), GFCI (ground-fault circuit interrupter), LGR (low grain refrigerant), and the scope sheet units.
- A few obscure short forms are written out in full: negative air machine, lockout/tagout, accelerated hydrogen peroxide, intellectual property, two Washington regional clean air agencies, and the Oregon environmental agency. A product code next to stain remover is dropped.
