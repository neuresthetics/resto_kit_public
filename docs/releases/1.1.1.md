- Every mention of the old name across the kit now points to Field Adaptations, including the pointer index, the on-request module list, and the pump truck voice note ("hope they can improvise it").

## New field fix: one-person chalk line

- Problem: snapping a chalk line alone, with no second person to hold the far end.
- Fix: put your knife blade through the hook ring on the end of the chalk line and stick the knife into the wall at your first mark. Pull the line all the way across to your other mark and snap it yourself.
- Result: a one-person chalk line.
