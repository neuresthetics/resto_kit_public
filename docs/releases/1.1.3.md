## Category 3 cargo liner

- On a Category 3 sewage job, before you load the van or truck with the extractor, hoses, or contaminated debris bags, line the cargo area with one large sheet of plastic.
- Run the plastic up one side wall, across the floor, and up the other side wall, like a bowl, with a raised lip at the back door.
- Dirty equipment and bags go in the lined area so a torn bag or dripping hose stays on the plastic instead of soaking into the vehicle.
- When the run is done, fold the plastic in on itself and dispose of it, or clean it as Category 3 contaminated material. That saves decontaminating the vehicle.
- This is not a default for ordinary demolition, because demo debris is solid.
