- **70**: Cat 3 marker sits between rows 69 and 70 because the photo is skewed. Best guess: Row 70 (pressure wash / under sill plates / extract). The row is shaded like other Cat 3 rows and the text slope places the marker there. Row 69 (clean joists) not flagged.
- **91**: Cat 3 column shows '#' rather than '3'. Best guess: Count marker (enter number of power boxes), not a Cat 3 flag. Treated cat3_flag=false.
- **87**: 'ETES' is an acronym, not spelled out. Best guess: Electric thermal energy system (heat drying). Confirm.
- **room cell 'A' marker**: Most room cells print a small checkbox at left and a letter 'A' at right; meaning not stated. Best guess: 'A' = all (entire room) shortcut instead of a measured quantity; the checkbox appears mainly on Cat 3 rows and may mean 'performed under Cat 3 conditions'. Not modeled per cell; confirm.
- **17, 19, 46, 48, 64, 72, 74**: No unit printed. Best guess: 17 QTY, 19 SF, 46 SF, 48 SF, 64 EA, 72 SF or HRS, 74 SF or HRS.
